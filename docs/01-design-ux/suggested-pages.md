## Mobile Theme Rules

For app screens, Claude must preserve these visual patterns:

- off-white background
- dark navy summary/header card near the top
- white stacked cards underneath
- bright blue primary button at the bottom of key flows
- orange callouts for live bidding, urgency, and savings
- slim iconography and small helper text
- rounded bottom tab bar with blue active item
